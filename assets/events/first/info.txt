Where all the philosophies come together
Seeking Occlusion Global Concept is an international symposium that brings together the most relevant  schools and specialists in the field of occlusion and oral rehabilitation of the masticatory system

A unique experience of professional growth
This 3-complete days long symposium will be your opportunity to get to know, understand and compare the main schools and understandings of occlusion and oral rehabilitation. The aim of the event is to exchange knowledge, clinical and technological achievements, as well as to compare the protocols of different schools of occlusion in the same space and time. 

This means that you will be able to know and compare different rehabilitation protocols and also get answers to your doubts during daily panel discussions. Such an opportunity occurs very rarely in dentistry.

In parallel to the lecture session, there will be an exhibition of innovative products and services on the dental market related to the diagnosis and treatment of the masticatory system, which will be an excellent opportunity for interaction between experts, professionals and companies from the dental industry.
